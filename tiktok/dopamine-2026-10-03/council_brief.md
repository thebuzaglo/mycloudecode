## The NEW video (being evaluated). 35.1s, 1080x1920
Built from a 5-beat script that a content app produced:
01 Opening: a clip from the previous video where he says "רק רציתי דופמין" + the text "מכורים לגרף?"
02 Short explanation: what "dopamine" means in trading: the thrill of feeling everything is going to plan, even when it isn't.
03 The danger: this urge leads to moving the stop-loss, adding contracts, and the shift from a "trader" mindset to a "gambler" one.
04 Advice: how to know yourself and separate the wish to succeed from the wish for a thrill. Comment prompt: "מכירים את ההרגשה?"
05 Close: a call to follow for more trading-psychology content.

How it was produced: Shlomi's face (a real photo) is lip-synced by an AI model (Wan 2.7) to an AI clone of his own voice (ElevenLabs). B-roll is AI-generated (Kling). The chart animation is motion graphics.
